Delete your Petfolio account

Petfolio is an app for your pet's diary and health record.

Delete in the app

Open Petfolio and go to: Settings → Legal & data → Delete account. The screen will ask you to enter the account email to confirm; an internet connection is required.

What will be deleted

  • Account profile
  • The entire diary of events and notes
  • Medical history and health record — vaccinations, visits, lab results, documents
  • Uploaded photos and attachments to entries and visits
  • Generated reports for the veterinarian
  • Active Petfolio+ subscription, if any — will stop working

Data is deleted from the cloud and from this device. This cannot be undone.

No access to the app?

Request deletion by email at terrorobado@gmail.com — write from the email address linked to your account.
